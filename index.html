Admission Details

Application Process

1

Online Application

Complete the online application form with required documents

2

Document Submission

Submit academic transcripts, certificates, and identification documents

3

Entrance Exam

Take the required entrance examination for your chosen faculty

4

Interview

Attend a personal interview with faculty representatives

5

Acceptance

Receive acceptance letter and complete registration

Requirements

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Minimum GPA of 3.0 (or equivalent)
  • English language proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS)
  • Valid passport or national ID
  • Medical examination certificate
  • Application fee payment
